Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to compare six real estate competitors across pricing, sales, leads, locations, and marketing performance for 2024.

Key Insights
- Market Leader: Crown Property Global Consult recorded the highest sales, leads, and conversion rate.
- Pricing Gap: Crown’s average listing price was ₦215M, compared with CityLink Properties at ₦130M.
- Top Location: Lekki recorded the highest sales, followed by Ikoyi and Victoria Island.
- Marketing Performance: Crown and Prime Estates recorded the highest commission revenue per unit of marketing spend, based on the dashboard figures.
  
Responsibilities and Achievements
- Collected, cleaned, and organized competitor data, preparing six competitors for comparative analysis.
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs and visualizations, making competitor performance easier to monitor.
- Analyzed pricing, leads, conversion rates, and sales performance, identifying market leaders and competitive gaps.
- Evaluated location and marketing performance, producing actionable recommendations for pricing, lead generation, and business growth.
